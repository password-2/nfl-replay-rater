How the ratings work

Quality rates how good a game was to watch: a shootout or a defensive battle, how much of the second half was within one score, lead changes, upsets, the finish, comebacks, big plays and defensive or special-teams scores. A great game can rate above 100%.

Stakes (0–100) measures before kickoff how much the result swung each team's chances of making the playoffs, winning the division or taking the 1 seed. High stakes move the verdict up a tier; nothing on the line moves it down one. Playoff games always count as high stakes.

Scores stay hidden until you tap Show score. Kickoff times are UK time. Data from ESPN, updated hourly.
